The firm

Law that understands the project—and the decision behind it.

Complex mandates rarely belong to a single legal category. A project-finance decision may turn on land, water, procurement, construction risk and governance at the same time.

We bring those interfaces together. The result is advice designed around the client's commercial objective, regulatory reality and route to implementation—not around isolated legal silos.

Decision-critical perspectives

Questions that deserve to be asked early.

PROJECT DELIVERY

A contract must work after signature.

We test obligations, interfaces, payment mechanics, change control and remedies through the delivery lens—contracts that are silent on project reality are costly.

REGULATORY STRATEGY

Sequencing can determine viability.

Licences, approvals, land, water, environment and procurement are considered together, not separately. The route to implementation often matters more than compliance alone.

TRANSACTION VALUE

Legal structure should protect the deal thesis.

Governance, funding, security, control, exit and downside protection are aligned to the value drivers that secured investment or lender support.
