Website privacy notice

Your information should be handled with the same care as your matter.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Prosper Nkala Inc. respects privacy and processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and other applicable South African law. This notice explains how personal information may be handled when you use this website or contact the firm.

1. Responsible party and contact details

The responsible party is Prosper Nkala Inc., Thornhill Office Park, 94 Bekker Road, Vorna Valley, Midrand, 1686, South Africa. Privacy enquiries may be sent to attorney@prospernkalainc.co.za or made by telephone on +27 10 023 2815.

2. Information we may process

Depending on your interaction with the firm, this may include:

  • identity, contact, organisation and role information;
  • the contents of enquiries, correspondence and documents you choose to provide;
  • matter, counterparty and transaction information required for conflict checks or legal services;
  • billing, payment, FICA and compliance information where a mandate is accepted; and
  • technical information ordinarily recorded by a website host, such as IP address, browser type and access logs.

3. Why information is processed

Personal information may be processed to respond to enquiries, perform conflict checks, decide whether the firm can accept a mandate, provide legal services, administer the client relationship, issue accounts, comply with legal and professional obligations, protect legal rights, maintain information security and communicate about relevant services where this is lawful.

4. Lawful processing

The firm processes information only where a ground recognised by POPIA or another law applies. Depending on the circumstances, this may include consent, steps required to conclude or perform a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, protection of a legitimate interest or performance of a public-law duty.

5. Sharing and cross-border processing

Information is not sold. It may be shared, only where appropriate, with personnel and service providers bound by confidentiality, advocates, experts, correspondent or foreign counsel, auditors, banks, insurers, courts, regulators and public authorities. Where information is transferred outside South Africa, the firm will apply the safeguards required by POPIA, including section 72 where applicable.

6. Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of personal information. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was obtained, the conduct or defence of legal proceedings, and applicable legal, tax, professional or record-keeping obligations.

7. Your rights

Subject to POPIA and other applicable law, you may request access to personal information, ask that inaccurate information be corrected, request deletion where legally permissible, object to particular processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis. The firm may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa).

8. Cookies and external services

This version of the website does not deliberately use advertising or analytics cookies and does not contain a web enquiry form. Your browser, network provider or website host may nevertheless create technical logs or use strictly necessary technologies under their own terms. Email and telephone links open the relevant application on your device.

9. Confidentiality of initial enquiries

Although the firm treats communications responsibly, sending an enquiry does not by itself establish an attorney-client relationship or confirm that the firm is able to act. Avoid sending highly sensitive material until a conflict check has been completed and the firm has confirmed the appropriate channel and mandate.

For privacy requests or questions about this notice, email attorney@prospernkalainc.co.za.
